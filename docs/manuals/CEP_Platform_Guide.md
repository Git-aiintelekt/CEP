# The CEP Platform Guide

This guide is for the platform team: the people who run the back-office at `/admin`, create workspaces for customers and keep the installation healthy. Workspace users have their own guide, the CEP User Guide.

## What the back-office does

| Area | What lives there |
|---|---|
| Tenants | Every workspace: onboarding (the six-step flow), its readiness panel, its industry profile and feature switches, its settings, its subscription and invoices, moving or exporting its data. |
| Plans and billing | The plans a workspace can be on, their limits, the monthly usage roll-up and the invoices it raises. |
| Users and roles | The platform's own staff accounts, and a view of every workspace's users and roles. |
| Configuration | Industry profiles, the platform's personalization tags inherited by every workspace, global parameters (typed fields), the platform's mail server. |
| Operations | Scheduled jobs and their runs, platform alerts, the health page, support sessions into a workspace, identity providers for single sign-on, the audit of definition changes. |
| Delivery | Every workspace's sending engines and the delivery log, read-only here. |
