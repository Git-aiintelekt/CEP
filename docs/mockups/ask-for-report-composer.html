What gets built, in words

  • One box in AskForReport.tsx: the thumbnails, the text area (no border of its own) and a bottom bar, inside one 12 px rounded border that turns blue with the focus ring while you type. The text grows with what you write and can still be resized.
  • Paperclip opens the file picker (Add Images). Its tooltip says Add Images; at 5 it is greyed out with "Five images is the most".
  • Drag and drop: while files are dragged anywhere over the Ask for a Report panel, the box shows the drop target (dashed Blue 600 border, light blue fill, "Drop images here", the types and the limit). Dropping anywhere on the panel adds them; leaving the panel or pressing Esc hides it. Pasting into the box keeps working.
  • Thumbnails 64 px, in the order added, each with ✕ and its file name under it; pointing at one shows the full name.
  • 5 images instead of 3: IMAGES_MAX = 5 in askImages.ts and in apps/insights/assistant.py. Each image keeps its 3.75 MB limit after it is scaled down, so a full request stays under 19 MB, inside Claude's and OpenAI's limits and the server's 50 MB.
  • Notes under the box in the same words as today: a file that is not PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP; too large; more than five ("Add up to 5 images. receipt-2.png was not added.").
  • No change to what is sent or kept: the images go to the model with the prompt and are not stored. No database patch.
  • Help: the Ask for a Report steps and "Add images to a prompt" say 5 and describe the drop target; the Word copies rebuilt.
  • Tests: dropping files on the panel, the five-image limit and the sixth refused, a non-image refused, the server refusing a sixth image.
Recommendation: Option A. It is the Claude prompt box you described: the prompt, its images, the model and the send button in one place, and the box reads as one thing to drop onto. Option B keeps today's order under the box, which is a smaller change but leaves the panel as two stacked parts.
