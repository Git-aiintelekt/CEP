Contact compliance. Three things, in the wizard and after it: a declared consent basis with an attestation on every upload, a workspace policy that decides what happens to people without consent, and a per-list record of all of it. The pictures below show the wizard step two ways (its own step, or folded into Review), then the pieces both share: the workspace policy, the approval gate on the list, and the compliance record. The send engine keeps deciding at send time; nothing here changes who a campaign reaches.

What gets built, in words

  • Upload: contact_list_import gains consent_basis, consent_collected_at (free text), attested_by / attested_at; the attestation sentence is a workspace parameter. A bought list cannot be chosen. A Texts list refuses the implied basis and a missing text consent column.
  • Policy: workspace parameters consent.without_consent (hold back | refuse), consent.texts_need_date, consent.approval_before_send, consent.lifetime_months. "Refuse" reports the rows on the review and the row-by-row CSV, exactly like an invalid address does today.
  • Gate: contact_list.approval_status (not required | awaiting | approved | refused) with who and when. The send engine's audience build skips a list that is awaiting or refused and names it in the run's hold-back reasons; the campaign's Send To table shows the tag.
  • Record: GET /contact-lists/{id}/compliance/ builds the page from the import, the approvals and today's consent records; PDF through the existing report renderer. Consent records written from the file carry the basis in their evidence.
  • Later, behind the switches that stay off: the double opt-in confirmation campaign, and the consent-lifetime reminder job.
