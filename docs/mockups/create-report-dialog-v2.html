The trail keeps step 2 one stepConnect and Upload are screens inside step 2, not new steps. The step line still says "Step 2 of 2", and the trail at the top (Choose Your Data › Connect a Database) shows where you are. Back goes one screen back.
Ask AI only with the subscriptionThe Ask AI tile shows only where the workspace has AI Reports (the same switch that puts AI Reports in the Insights menu). Without it, Start from Data, Use Base Reports and Write a Query sit in one row of three.
Who sees Connect and UploadOnly people who can build reports, as today. Someone without that right sees just the datasets.
Built from what existsThe connection form, the table picker and the column review are today's components (DatabaseConnectionForm, the drawer's table step, UploadReviewDialog), moved into the pop-up. No backend change.
