Engine Health — watching Postal and Digivize for you. Mockup, not the product. C360 already records every call to a sending engine (how long it took, what came back) and every callback, but nobody is told when an engine stops answering, stops calling back or starts bouncing. Engine Health checks each active engine of each workspace every 10 minutes and raises an alert through the existing alert channels (email to the platform team, the webhook). Below: the plan in words, then three options for where it shows, each drawn busy and sparse. Back-office parts use the back-office's own look; workspace parts use the app's tokens.

The plan in words

Five checks per engine, every 10 minutes

CheckRaises whenSeverity
CallbacksThe engine took messages in the last hour, but no callback has arrived for more than 60 minutesFailing
RejectionsMore than 1% of calls in the last hour were refused; any 401 or 403 (the key was refused); the circuit breaker is openFailing (key refused, breaker); Attention (rate)
API speedThe engine's 95th-percentile answer time over the last hour is above 5 sAttention
Bounces and complaintsEmail, last 24 h: hard bounces above 2% or complaints above 0.1%. Texts: failures above 5% of receiptsAttention; Failing at twice the line
Receipts (texts)Texts still without a delivery receipt 15 minutes after they were sentAttention
  • Rates need enough traffic: a rate only counts from 50 messages (or calls) in its window, so one bounce out of three test sends never alerts.
  • Digivize, said plainly: Digivize accepts texts but has never posted a delivery receipt from its sandbox. Until an engine has sent its first receipt, the Receipts check does not count missing receipts; it shows one standing line, “Digivize has not sent delivery receipts yet: texts show Sent, not Delivered”, and opens one alert that is emailed once and not repeated. The first receipt that arrives switches the real check on by itself.
  • An idle engine is Quiet, not Failing: no traffic in the window means nothing to judge.
  • Daily Check: a new job runs each active engine's Check once a day (the same call as the Check link: key and address, nothing sent); a failed Check is an alert.
  • Alerts: one alert per engine and check, e.g. Engine · NWA · Postal Marketing · Callbacks, through the existing alert evaluator: notified at once, again every 4 h while it lasts (unless acknowledged), resolved with a notice when it clears.
  • Thresholds are Global Parameters under engine_health.* (callback silence minutes, rejection %, p95 ms, hard-bounce %, complaint %, SMS failure %, receipt wait minutes, minimum sample), so the platform team tunes them without a release.
  • Storage: the latest result per engine in one small table (delivery_provider_health), so pages read it instead of recounting. Its patch file goes in database/patches/ and is applied only on your “apply patch”.
  • Jobs: check-engine-health (every 10 minutes) and check-engines-daily (once a day), registered like the others, so Home's Scheduled jobs watches them too.

Option A · Back-office onlyA new page, Operations → Engine Health, lists every active engine of every workspace with the five checks; problems also appear on Overview → Alerts and in Home's “Needs attention”. Workspaces see nothing new. Best if engines are run for the workspaces by the platform team.

Colours and words

Health tags reuse the status tag shapes without a dot (health is not a live lifecycle status): Healthy uses the Active green, Attention and No Receipts Yet the Paused yellow (“needs a decision”), Failing the Failed rose, Quiet neutral grey. In the back-office the existing Unfold pills are used, as on the Alerts page.

Recommendation

Option C. The alerts land with the platform team, who need one page across all workspaces to see whether a problem is one engine or Postal itself (Option A). But the fix is often in the workspace's hands (registering the callback address, replacing a refused key, cleaning a list that bounces), and today the Sending Engines page already shows the callback line, so a Health tag with a See Health drawer there costs little and says what to do. B alone leaves the platform team without the cross-workspace view; A alone leaves the workspace guessing why texts say Sent.
