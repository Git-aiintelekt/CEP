Scheduled delivery, mocked up (Phase E). A report or a dashboard goes out by email on a schedule: every day, every weekday, weekly on a day, or monthly on a day, at a time in the workspace's zone, to a list of addresses, as Excel, CSV or PDF, with a subject and a note. The two options differ in where that is set up: from the report itself, with a list to keep an eye on them all; or from one Deliveries page where an email can carry several reports. What is sent, how a failure is handled and the run log are the same in both. Data: Northwind Apparel, October 2026.

Option 1 · Schedule From the Report
The Schedule dialog, from a report's viewer (and a dashboard's)Schedule sits beside Export on the viewer. One report per schedule; the dialog is one screen. The format offers what the report can be: Excel, CSV, PDF; a dashboard offers PDF only. Recipients are typed addresses; people of the workspace complete as you type.

What is the same in both options

  • Every day, every weekday, weekly on a day, or monthly on a day, at a time in the workspace's zone. The scheduler checks every five minutes; a delivery that missed its minute goes out on the next pass, never twice.
  • The file is built by the engine with the rights and masking of whoever scheduled it, at the moment of sending: the report as it is saved then, in its saved view. A dashboard goes as PDF, on its saved period.
  • Sent through the workspace's own mail server (Settings → Mail Server); a workspace without one cannot schedule, and the dialog says so.
  • Each delivery is a run in the Run Log, kind Delivery. Three failures in a row pause the schedule and tell its owner by email; Resume starts it again.
  • Send a Test Now sends the email to the person setting it up, at once.
  • Tables: report_schedule (what, when, to whom, as what, status, last and next) and report_delivery (each sending). Patch 0052.

Recommendation: Option 1 for the first slice. It puts the action where people are, on the report, and the list keeps the overview; packs can follow as a second kind of schedule without changing what is saved.
