Journey Date / Time trigger — Set-Up step 1 (mockup, not the product). Today Date / Time saves only the word “Daily” or “Weekly”: no time, no day, no audience, so it enrols nobody. A time-based entry needs to know when (a time, plus a weekday, a day of the month or a date) and who (a customer list or a segment). The fifth choice, On a Date Attribute, works per customer from a date on their record (birthday, membership date) with an offset in days. Two ways to lay this out, each in a simple case and a busy case. The rest of the pop-up (name, step 2) is unchanged.

Option 1 — stacked fields under Specific TriggerUses the Field primitives the pop-up already has. Fields appear for the schedule chosen; a grey summary line at the bottom reads the result back in words. Same pattern as the Segment picker that appears for Enters Segment today.

What gets built, in words

The same for both options; only the layout differs.

The choices

Specific TriggerAsks forWho enters, and when
DailyTime; who entersThe list or segment’s members, every day at that time.
WeeklyOne or more weekdays; time; who entersThe members, on those days at that time.
MonthlyDay of the month (1–28, or Last Day); time; who entersThe members, once a month.
On a Specific DateDate; time; who entersThe members, once, at that moment.
On a Date AttributeWhich date on the customer’s record; on the day, or 1–90 days before or after; time; Every Year or Once; optionally a list or segmentEach customer on their own date. Every Year matches day and month (29 Feb counts on 28 Feb).

Saved and run

  • The entry rule keeps schedule and adds time, weekdays, day_of_month, date, attribute, offset_days, yearly and the audience (contact_list_id or segment_id). No new table; one patch adds journey_version.last_fired_at so a moment fires once.
  • The enrolment job (every minute) fires a schedule when its moment has passed since last_fired_at: the audience enters with that moment as the time they qualified. Re-entry from step 2 applies as it does today, so Weekly with “After 7 days” lets the same people back each week.
  • Times are the workspace’s time zone. A later option can follow each customer’s own zone, as campaigns’ local-time sending does.
  • Date attributes come from the customer record’s custom attributes that hold dates (CEP has no birthday column). A small read endpoint lists those keys with how many customers have each, for the Date drop-down. Until C360 sends a birthday, On a Date Attribute offers only what is there (for example Last Purchase).
  • Test Publish’s random sample uses the same rule: who would enter at the next moment.
  • Population mode: Fixed fires once and closes; the other two keep firing.

Recommendation

Option 1. It reuses the pop-up’s own Field controls and the pattern already there for Enters Segment, works at phone width, and every choice is a labelled control for keyboard and screen readers. The summary line gives Option 2’s read-back without its pop-overs.
