Navy Rail Pages

The navy rail now holds the open product’s pages, and the white menu column is gone. Products are switched only in the 9-dot launcher. Every frame is clickable: the toggle collapses and expands, the dots open the launcher, a launcher tile switches the product, and collapsed icons show their label on hover. Approved 2026-11-20: the product’s name heads the rail (no subline), Insights stays one flat list, Contact Lists in title case.

1 · Engagement, Expanded

xl and up: the rail is 240px, open unless the person collapsed it (remembered, as today). Icon and label side by side, the current page on the navy selected fill with white semibold text. Settings stays at the foot under a divider. Content gains 88px over today (240 instead of 88 + 240).

2 · Engagement, Collapsed

The top bar’s toggle shrinks the rail to a 64px column of 40px icon squares; each label is a dark tooltip (shown here on Journeys). Same as today’s collapsed white menu, now on navy.

3 · The Launcher, the Only Product Switcher

Unchanged in content: “Find C360 apps”, one tile per product the workspace has (current one marked, a trial shows “Trial to …”), and the “coming” line. Picking a tile opens that product’s Home and swaps the rail’s pages.

4 · Insights, Expanded

Insights’ ten pages fit in one column on a 720px-tall window (10 × 44px plus Settings). Items the person can’t use are left out, as today (AI Reports only with AI on; Run Log and Usage for report admins).

5 · Insights, Collapsed

6 · Below xl (1024px): Opened Over the Page

Below xl the rail sits collapsed at 64px; the toggle opens it to 240px over the page with the scrim, never pushing the page (today’s behaviour). On phones the toggle opens the 240px rail over the page the same way.

Kept, Moved or Changed

  • Kept: every page link, permission check, the Journeys feature flag, the AI-gated AI Reports item, Settings for workspace admins, the toggle and its remembered state, tooltips when collapsed, the launcher’s search, tiles and “coming” line.
  • Moved: product switching (rail tiles) goes into the launcher only. The rail’s amber “Trial” tag moves beside the top-bar name; the launcher keeps “Trial to …”.
  • Dropped (your call): the subline under the product name. The name heads the expanded rail; the tagline stays only as the launcher tile’s tooltip and in its search.
  • Gone: the white 240px menu column and the 88px product rail.
