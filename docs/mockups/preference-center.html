3 · The process

What happens between the email and the database.
1
Link in the emailThe template carries {{preference_url}}. At send time it becomes a link with the same signed token as the unsubscribe link: workspace, contact, campaign, run, address. Test sends get a token marked test.
Reuses unsubscribe.token_for
2
Page opensGET /api/v1/public/preferences/?t=… The workspace is read from the token, the person's current choices from consent and topic tables. A view is recorded.
tracking_event PREFERENCE_VIEW
3
Choices savedPOST writes one consent record per change and updates the current state. Channel off = opt-out; channel on = opt-in (if the workspace allows it); topics = the person's topic list.
consent_record source PREFERENCE_CENTER
4
Sending honours itChannel choices already apply at every audience build. The topic filter is one more governance rule: a campaign filed under a topic leaves out everyone who unticked it.
New: campaign.topic_id, party_topic_preference
5
Visible in the workspaceSuppressions shows the method Preference Center; the contact's page shows their topics; Settings → Preference Centre shows the counts.
Patch: 2 tables, 1 column
Suggested order: v1 the tag, the page with channel switches, "unsubscribe from all" and opting in again, the Settings page without topics, the link from the unsubscribe confirmation (one PR, no topic tables yet). v2 topics: the table and Settings section, then the campaign field and the audience filter (two PRs, one patch).
