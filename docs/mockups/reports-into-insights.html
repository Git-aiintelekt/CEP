Engagement Reports in Insights

Mockup only. Nothing is built yet. Names and numbers are made up. It uses the app's own colours, type and spacing; the shell is drawn smaller than life and without its icons.

Why: Engagement has its own Reports page, built before Insights existed. It draws fixed figures: nobody can add a column, save a view, share it or put it on a dashboard. Insights can do all of that from the same campaign records. Moving the page means drawing the same figures as Insights reports on one dashboard, Engagement Overview, and then deciding where a person finds it.

Nothing is lost. Every figure on today's page is on the dashboard: the four key figures, Sent and Opened by Day, Best and Worst, Campaigns in the Period and the Journeys table. Compare, Export CSV and Schedule Report become Insights' own Compare Campaigns report, Export to Excel and Scheduled.

Three options below. The dashboard is the same in all three; they differ in the menu: A sends Reports to Insights, B keeps the Reports page in Engagement and lets Insights draw it, C takes Reports out of Engagement's menu. Each is shown busy (a month of sends) and sparse (a workspace that has sent nothing yet).

The same in every option: the new reports and the dashboard are built first and shown beside today's page until their figures are confirmed to match. Only then does the old page go, with its four API addresses. Today's page reads ready-made figures; the dashboard runs a query per tile, so it keeps its answers for 15 minutes like every Insights report, with Refresh beside the time.

Recommendation: B. It moves the figures to Insights, which is the point, without moving the people. A workspace that has Engagement but not Insights keeps its reports, nobody's links or help break, and Open in Insights is there for the few who want to change or schedule something. A is the natural second step once every workspace has Insights; C fits when the suite has several components and Insights is where everyone already looks.
