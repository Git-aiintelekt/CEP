Only a platform administrator sees it. You enter the other workspace as yourself, with full rights (not as one of its users, unlike a support session), until you go back or sign out. What gets built, in words

  • Who: platform administrators only (is_platform_admin), with two-step verification when the platform requires it for staff. Nobody else sees the switch.
  • How it works: the server records a visit and issues a token for the chosen workspace in your own name, marked as a visit; the app reloads in that workspace. There you have full rights, as the workspace's own administrator has; you leave with Back to Platform or by signing out.
  • The record: a platform_visit row per visit (who, workspace, from, until, reason in option B), kept in the platform's records like support sessions; the workspace's administrators read theirs under Settings → Security → Platform Visits; the back-office lists all of them. Patch 0077.
  • The list: every workspace, recent first, searchable by code or name, with plan and status; suspended and offboarding ones are shown with their status.
  • Help and tests: a "Switch Workspace" step in the platform guide; tests for who sees it, the visit record, Back to Platform, and the administrators' list.
Recommendation: Option A. The top bar already names the workspace, so it is the natural place to change it, and it is one click for the super user who moves between Platform and Demo many times a day. Every visit is still recorded with its times. Choose Option B if a written reason should be required each time, as support sessions require one.
