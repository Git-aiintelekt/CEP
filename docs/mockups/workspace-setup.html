Workspace set-up, from the administrator's side. The back-office onboards the tenant and emails the first administrator a one-time password link with the set-up list. From there everything happens in the app: they choose a password, sign in, and land on Set Up Your Workspace, a page that knows what is done and what is not, and sends them to the right Settings page for each step. The page stays reachable from Home until the workspace is ready; nothing on it is a new form, it is a guide over the pages that exist.

Ready = steps 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6. The page is reachable at Settings → Set-Up for every administrator; it lands there on the first sign-in only while the workspace is not ready, and the Home card shows until ready or dismissed (dismissal is per workspace, kept in its settings).

What gets built, in words

  • The facts: GET /workspace/setup/ answers the seven steps with done / state / detail / link, computed from users and roles, delivery providers and their last check, sender profiles, suppressions, contact lists, notification settings, approvals and API keys; plus ready and dismissed. PUT dismisses the Home card (tenant.settings.setup.dismissed_at).
  • The page (Option 1): /settings/setup in the Settings menu's Workspace group, administrators only; each step a row with its button to the page that does it. The first sign-in of an administrator lands there while ready is false (the login redirect reads the same endpoint).
  • The Home card: under Needs Attention, for administrators, until ready or dismissed: "Set up your workspace · n of 7 done · Next: …".
  • The wizard (Option 2) would wrap the existing forms (Users invite, Add Engine, Add Sender, the upload wizard) in one modal; the readiness logic is the same.
  • Unchanged: the back-office hand-off email already lists the steps; the campaign Review step keeps its own checks before publishing.
